A Word from Fr. Robert Rego

Dear Konkani Kutam Family,

When we left our homeland and made Germany our home, we carried with us more than just our luggage, we brought our language, our faith, our traditions, our values, and the rich heritage of our Konkani community. Konkani Kutam Germany was founded with a simple yet meaningful vision: to give every Konkani family a home away from home. A place where we celebrate our festivals together, speak our mother tongue with pride, pass on our customs to the next generation, and strengthen the bonds that unite us as one community. As we preserve our roots, let us also embrace the country that has welcomed us with open arms. Germany has given us opportunities, security, and the means to provide for our families. It is our responsibility to respect its laws, honour its culture, contribute positively to society, and give back through our hard work, integrity, and community spirit. Our identity is enriched when we remain firmly rooted in our Konkani heritage while becoming responsible and respected members of German society. These two are not in conflict—they complement one another beautifully. Let us ensure that our children know where they come from, cherish their faith and traditions, and at the same time grow into compassionate, responsible citizens who appreciate the values of the country they call home. Together, let us continue building a vibrant Konkani community in Germany—one that celebrates its roots, strengthens its faith, preserves its culture, and contributes wholeheartedly to the society in which we live. May God continue to bless our families, our community, and both the lands we are privileged to call home.

With warm regards,

— Fr. Rego Robert

Our Mission

To unite Konkani Catholics residing in Germany under one umbrella, preserving our Culture, and language while supporting one another in building a fulfilling life in Germany.

🌟

Our Vision

A thriving Konkani diaspora community in Germany where every generation from grandparents to grandchildren, feels a proud, living connection to our Konkani identity.

⚖️

Our Values

Inclusivity, respect, faith, volunteerism, and the Mangalorean spirit of hospitality — aamogelan. Every member is family.
