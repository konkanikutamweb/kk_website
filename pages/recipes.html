Pork Bafat (Dukramass)

The signature staple dish of the pork-loving Mangalorean — tender pork belly slow-cooked with aromatic bafat spices, tangy tamarind, and vinegar. Tastes even better the next day, once the flavours have had time to develop.

🐖 Pork Belly 🌶️ Mangalorean Bafat 🍲 Slow-Cooked

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly, cubed
  • 3 green chillies, slit
  • 2 inches ginger, finely chopped
  • 2 inches cinnamon
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 tbsp bafat powder
  • Lemon-sized tamarind, soaked
  • Salt, to taste
  • 4 large onions, cubed
  • 1 pod garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Optional: 250g radish, turnip, yam or sweet potato

Method

  1. Marinate: Combine the pork with green chillies, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, bay leaf, bafat powder, and salt. Let it marinate for at least 1 hour, up to 4 hours for best results.
  2. Cook: Heat the marinated pork on a low flame with the tamarind water and pulp. Cover and cook until the meat is nearly done, stirring occasionally without adding extra water.
  3. Add vegetables: Once the meat is almost cooked, add the onions, garlic, and any optional vegetables. Stir well and continue cooking until everything is tender.
  4. Finish: Stir in the vinegar and cook for a few more minutes before serving. Even better enjoyed the next day!
